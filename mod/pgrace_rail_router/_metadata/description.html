Rail Router

Pick two towns and Rail Router plans a double-track line between them, with stations as close to each town centre as possible without demolishing anything.

Features

  • Cheapest or Fastest: track that follows the terrain to save money, or runs straight and flat for speed.
  • Stops on the way: towns close to the line are offered as extra stops, each with the detour it adds. Untick the ones you don't want.
  • No demolition: stations go on clear, flat ground as near each town centre as possible, and the route goes round buildings.
  • The numbers before you build: length, bridges and tunnels, the steepest grade, the tightest curve and a cost estimate, with warnings where the route needs your attention.
  • Preview on the map: the route is drawn as arrows (bridges and tunnels in their own colours), with the camera framed on it.
  • Choose the track type, electrification and platform length.

How to use

  1. Click the train button at the top left of the screen, next to the map-layers button.
  2. Choose the two towns under From and To, and pick a mode.
  3. Press Find route and wait for the survey and planning to finish.
  4. Press Show on map to see the route.

Good to know

  • This version plans and previews only; building the line with one click comes in a later version.
  • The cost is the mod's own estimate for the track, not the game's price.
  • This is a cosmetic mod. It doesn't change the simulation, so achievements stay available, and you can add it to or remove it from existing saves.
